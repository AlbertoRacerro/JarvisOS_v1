# 216 — Design Phase 2: smooth-tube CFD vertical slice

Accepted outcome and readiness: maintainer [issue #901](https://github.com/AlbertoRacerro/JarvisOS_v1/issues/901) decisions 5, 6, 7 and 9 and its "Order of work" step 2, scheduled after Phase 1 (spec 214) and the C1 follow-up (spec 215) by the [#901 scheduling update](https://github.com/AlbertoRacerro/JarvisOS_v1/issues/901#issuecomment-6364677846). The issue fixes the product direction and the engine choice (OpenFOAM only, Gmsh, vtk.js/PyVista); this contract fixes the bounded Phase 2 outcome and evidence. Live state is only in `STATUS.md`.

## Outcome

Inside the spec 214 Design shell, an engineer opens the component of a Process pipe/tube unit, derives its internal fluid domain from the parametric BlueCad smooth tube, meshes it, runs a pinned OpenFOAM study from a typed contract, inspects convergence and fields in the component viewport, and explicitly publishes a versioned reduced model (pressure drop versus Reynolds number, with validity range and uncertainty). The Process unit pins that version and uses it. When the tube geometry or an upstream study input changes, Process shows the pinned reduced model as obsolete and asks for a rerun; it never changes silently. Target: ready before C2/C3, which need hydraulics and shear.

## Contract

1. **Toolchain.** OpenFOAM (one pinned ESI or Foundation release) and Gmsh are available to the backend through the existing tool registry (specs 008 and 014), with recorded version, binary/package digest, provenance and licence. They are installed outside the repository in a project tool location reachable from `jarvis-sandbox`, or through a typed host operation. Absent tools make the study report *unavailable* with the reason, never a fabricated result. The registry pin may move from the historical `v2412` value to the release actually installed; the change is recorded. Known release defects (for example function-object failures) are worked around inside the adapter and documented.
2. **Fluid domain and mesh.** The fluid domain is derived deterministically from the component's parametric BlueCad smooth-tube geometry (diameter, length, and the assembly's single fluid volume where the spec 214 model allows one). It carries a geometry digest. Meshing uses Gmsh and/or OpenFOAM's own mesher with named physical groups (inlet, outlet, wall, and symmetry planes when a reduced 2D-axisymmetric or periodic domain is used). A mesh check (cell count, non-orthogonality, skewness, aspect ratio, and for RANS an estimated wall y⁺) is recorded and can refuse the run. A reduced domain is acceptable when its equivalence to the 3D tube is stated and tested.
3. **Typed case generation.** A pydantic study contract (geometry reference, fluid density and viscosity, inlet velocity or flow rate, outlet pressure, regime, turbulence model and wall treatment, mesh level, solver tolerances) deterministically generates the complete case (`0/`, `constant/`, `system/`). Dictionaries are produced only from typed values; no free-form or agent-written dictionary text reaches the solver. Boundary-condition consistency and regime/model compatibility are validated before execution. Each case has a manifest of input digests and the pinned tool version.
4. **Execution and observability.** Runs go through the existing evaluator/study owners (specs 014 and 110) and the spec 210 solver console: live residuals parsed while the solver runs, convergence diagnostics, safe stop, versioned retry and run comparison. Buffered final logs are never shown as live streaming. Runs execute outside the request thread with bounded time and workspace, and artifacts land in runtime storage outside the repository.
5. **Verification study.** The slice ships a pipe study that compares (a) laminar developed flow at Re within about 100–1000 against Hagen–Poiseuille pressure gradient and the parabolic profile, (b) mass conservation, (c) a mesh-sensitivity study on at least three meshes with an observed order or GCI-style uncertainty, and (d) at least one turbulent smooth-pipe case at the C1-like condition (D = 46 mm, v ≈ 0.45 m/s, water-like culture, Re ≈ 2×10⁴) with a standard RANS model against Blasius/Colebrook and a stated model/wall-treatment limitation. Tolerances are stated before the comparison and reported honestly when missed.
6. **Results in the viewport.** The component's Results node shows the run's convergence history and at least a pressure and velocity field rendering from a VTK export (vtk.js in the browser, PyVista for server-side checks), plus the computed ΔP/L and wall shear. The Mesh and Studies nodes stop being placeholders for this tube; other components keep the honest spec 214 placeholder labels.
7. **Reduced-model publication.** From the validated study the engineer explicitly publishes a versioned reduced model: ΔP (or Darcy friction factor) versus Re over the validated range, validity bounds (Re, D, fluid properties, regime), uncertainty from the mesh study and the correlation error, and provenance (study runs, geometry digest, contract digest, tool version). Publication is an operation with its own version identity, reusing existing version/pin owners (Studio published versions and the 109/110 handoff) rather than a new store. Unpublished or failing studies cannot be published.
8. **Process use and obsolescence.** A Process pipe/tube unit can switch from its literature 0D/1D correlation to a pinned published reduced-model version and shows its fidelity level. Evaluating outside the validity range warns or refuses visibly. When the component geometry digest or a pinned upstream study input changes, the unit shows "reduced model obsolete, rerun the study" and keeps the pinned version until the engineer acts. No live co-simulation.
9. **One command layer.** Fluid-domain derivation, mesh, run study, stop/retry and publish exist as typed backend operations used by both the GUI and authorized agent tools, writing the shared spec 214 component history with attribution.
10. **Prototype input.** The cloud-lane prototype of [issue #913](https://github.com/AlbertoRacerro/JarvisOS_v1/issues/913) is read as input once its evidence exists, then adopted, adapted or discarded through this contract. Its branch/PR remains the maintainer's; this slice does not modify, rebase or merge it.

## Acceptance and evidence

1. Offline tests (no OpenFOAM required) cover contract validation and refusals, deterministic case generation with stable manifests, mesh-check refusal, log/residual parsing, reduced-model fitting/validity/uncertainty, publication guards, Process pinning, out-of-range behaviour and obsolescence after a geometry or input change. Tool-dependent tests skip cleanly when the binaries are absent.
2. A real-tool evidence run on the workstation (sandbox or a typed host operation with its receipt id) executes the verification study of contract 5 from one command and records versions, digests, mesh table, observed order/GCI, laminar and turbulent errors versus the analytic/correlation references, and the published reduced model. An implementation evidence document reports the numbers, plots and limits.
3. A real-browser walk in Lumen Light and Dark shows Plant → tube unit → component → CFD stage → mesh → run with live residuals and stop → fields and ΔP/L in the viewport → publish → Process unit pinned to the reduced model with its fidelity label → geometry change → obsolescence warning → rerun and republish. Capture screenshots and console errors.
4. Ruff, relevant backend tests, frontend build/tests and an exact-head semantic review of numerics, provenance and claims pass. The C1 parity of spec 214 and the 215 band regression stay green.

## Boundaries

- Smooth straight tube only. No helical insert, Smart Joint, multiphase/gas–liquid, mass transfer, heat transfer, optics coupling, FSI, peristaltic pump model, Motion/Mechanics content or C2–C9 science; those are later slices.
- No Kratos or second CFD engine, no solver benchmarking, no commercial-licence work. No new canonical database or workflow engine; reuse the registry, evaluator, study, solver-console, version-pin and component-history owners.
- The result is a qualified reduced model within stated validity, never a *validated* plant claim. CFD output does not silently replace Process design authority.
- Preserve current user data, existing API consumers, the old Design behind the switch, Studio sandbox and AI safety defaults. Runtime data and tool installations stay outside the repository.
- Phase 2 may be delivered through several coherent implementation PRs under this single contract (for example toolchain + case generation, study + publication, Process pinning + UI). Every PR keeps `STATUS.md` truthful; final acceptance requires the integrated browser walk and the real-tool evidence run. Planned specs 176 and 179 keep their wider scope; this slice delivers only their smooth-tube CFD, publication and staleness subset.
