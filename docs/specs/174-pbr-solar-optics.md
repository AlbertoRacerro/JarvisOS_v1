## Decision

1. **One pure kernel, `process_stack/optics_t2.py`.** Deterministic, vectorized numpy, no I/O, no global state, imports nothing from the engine. Inputs: sun elevation and azimuth, DNI and DHI as PAR (already converted), tube diameter D, tube-centre list in the cross-section plane, axis azimuth, `n_wall`, `n_water`, sea albedo, culture extinction. Outputs per tube. Nothing else is added to the engine except its hand-off (decisions 7-8).
2. **Geometry (2D cross-section plus axial angle).** Tubes are infinite parallel cylinders of equal D with horizontal axis at azimuth alpha. For unit vector s to the source: axial component a = s.axis, cos(gamma) = sqrt(1 - a^2), in-plane direction psi. A beam strip of lateral width w and length L carries power DNI*cos(gamma)*w*L, so an isolated tube normal to a beam intercepts DNI*D per metre. A tube is **layout-described** by `tubes_across`, `layers`, `pitch_h`, `pitch_v`, optional stagger offset, `axis_azimuth`, `centre_height_above_sea`; The layout is a **representative cross-section module** of `tubes_across*layers` tubes, at most 64. `tube_count` (the parallel tubes that set volume and circulation, `pbr_unit.py:813-818`) may be larger. Every tube receives the module-mean absorbed PAR, so edge effects are represented at the module's scale, and this is recorded as a caveat. `tube_count` must be at least the module tube count, and a module larger than 64 tubes is refused.
3. **Inter-tube shading is exact 2D projected-shadow geometry, opaque.** Tube j shadows tube i for a source direction when j lies on the source side of i along s_perp and their silhouette intervals (width D, centred at the projections onto the normal of s_perp) overlap. The unshadowed set of i is its silhouette interval minus the union of upstream intervals, computed exactly (sorted breakpoints, no grid). Upstream tubes are treated as **opaque**, which is a stated lower bound (light through an upstream culture is ignored). Identity used as a test: the sum over tubes of unshadowed width equals the measure of the union of all silhouettes.
4. **Fresnel wall.** Unpolarised, thin wall (thickness ignored), air / wall (`n_wall`) / water (`n_water`), incoherent multiple reflections: T_slab = T1*T2/(1 - R1*R2) per polarisation, averaged; no wall absorption. Local incidence angle at silhouette offset s: cos(theta) = cos(gamma)*sqrt(1 - (s/R)^2). The wall-weighted visible width integrates T(theta) over the unshadowed intervals by fixed Gauss-Legendre per interval. `n_wall` and `n_water` are explicit inputs (wavelength-free, PAR-mean); there is no hidden default, as in 170.
5. **Sources.** Beam from DNI at the pvlib sun vector. Isotropic sky, radiance DHI/pi over the upper hemisphere (no circumsolar or anisotropic sky; the circumsolar part is in DNI). Sea-reflected light: isotropic lower hemisphere, radiance rho*GHI/pi with GHI = DNI*sin(e) + DHI and a constant `sea_albedo` rho. No sea glint or angle-dependent water reflectance (declared residual). Diffuse and sea terms depend only on layout and axis azimuth, so they are integrated once per run (fixed hemispherical quadrature using decision 3 and 4 for each direction) and scaled by DHI and rho*GHI per step.
6. **Culture attenuation stays T1 Beer-Lambert.** Absorbed PAR per unit length = sum over rays of T*(1 - exp(-kappa*l)), with straight refracted chords l = D*cos(phi_w)/cos(gamma_w) (Snell with tangential wavenumber conserved; reduces to D*cos(phi)/cos(gamma) at n = 1). Internal re-reflection is ignored (lower bound). **Two-flux scattering is a non-goal.** A rigorous two-flux needs a measured scattering coefficient that no model card carries (Q2 itself says its value depends on measured radiative properties), and a plane two-flux slab does not match the cylindrical field that 170 certified. A user may write it as a Studio model.
7. **Selection.** New per-PBR parameter `optics_model: "t1" | "t2"`, default `t1`. The T2-only parameters of decision 2, 4 and 5 are required only when `t2`; with `t1` the stored unit, its digest, the cache key, result and the 172 manifest are byte-identical to master (pinned by a golden test). `t2` applies to the 172 dynamic engine only; the 170 periodic-steady evaluator refuses it with `OPTICS_T2_REQUIRES_DYNAMIC_PROFILE`.
8. **Engine hand-off.** At `prepare`, for each `t2` PBR the engine computes the optics once per profile interval (sun vector at the interval midpoint; pvlib `spa_python` with the site snapshot) and stores per-interval arrays beside `par`. In `rhs` it only indexes them, never calls the kernel. The growth law still evaluates T1's certified field: the kernel supplies `I0_eq` = lim(kappa->0) absorbed/(kappa*pi*R^2) (thin-limit mean scalar irradiance) and diffuse share `f_d`. `Growth.light` gains an optional per-call diffuse-fraction argument; the default path is unchanged. The profile needs `dni` and `dhi`; a profile lacking them, a missing site, or an interval over the resolution limit is refused (`OPTICS_CHANNELS_MISSING`, `OPTICS_SITE_MISSING`, `OPTICS_RESOLUTION`). The resolution limit is frozen from a measured midpoint-sun bias table at 5, 15 and 60 min (Cadiz solstices), not guessed here. GHI-only decomposition is not provided.
9. **Fidelity.** A `t2` unit reports "T2 - unqualified - profile-driven tube/array interception, Fresnel wall, opaque shading, no energy balance"; the manifest `fidelity` is `T2` when any unit is `t2`, else unchanged `T1`, and each unit records `optics: {model, version, parameter digest, pvlib version, site digest}`. `Scenario.fidelity` input stays `T1`. New series per `t2` unit (names registered in the unit mapping at `dynamic_engine.py:2398`): `<tag>_par_intercepted_umol_s_m`, `<tag>_par_absorbed_umol_s_m`, `<tag>_par_eq_umol_m2_s`, `<tag>_beam_unshaded_fraction`.
10. **Optics model kept out of the model card.** It is a unit parameter, not a 169 card field; card qualification labels are untouched. Nothing in 174 is labelled qualified.

## Non-goals and explicit residuals

- No ray tracing, no re-reflection between tubes or off the sea, no spectral resolution beyond one PAR fraction (the engine's existing conversion factor), no CFD or optical look-up tables (179), no two-flux (decision 6), no GHI decomposition or tilted-plane transposition, no anisotropic sky, no sea glint or wave focusing, no tube bends, finite tube ends or support shading, no partially submerged tubes, no energy balance (175).
- Opaque shadows and ignored internal reflection make absorbed PAR a conservative estimate; the sign of each simplification is documented in the manifest caveats.
- The growth law keeps T1's unrefracted field shape. The difference between the exact refracted absorption and the T1-shape absorption at the same `I0_eq` is measured over a tau grid and printed in the evidence, with its tolerance frozen after measurement.

## Acceptance

1. **Analytic single tube.** Sun at the zenith or normal to an isolated tube, `n_wall = n_water = 1`: intercepted = DNI*D per metre exactly (rtol 1e-12); with a real wall: DNI*D*T with T the closed-form Fresnel slab value (n = 1.5 normal: 0.96 for one interface). Isolated diffuse intercept = (pi/2)*D*DHI (from the integral of cos(gamma) over the upper hemisphere, pi^2/2); the sea term likewise (pi/2)*D*rho*GHI.
2. **Fresnel.** R + T = 1 at all angles; T(0) closed form; grazing T -> 0; n_wall = n_water reduces to a single interface; the unpolarised average equals the mean of the two Fresnel polarisations to quadrature tolerance.
3. **Shading.** Single layer, sun at the zenith: no shading; stacked layers at zenith: only the top tube sees the beam; in-row grazing sun: only the upstream-most tube is lit; the union identity of decision 3 on 10^4 random directions and layouts; every unshadowed width in [0, D]; symmetry under layout mirroring; monotone non-increase as pitch decreases.
4. **Conservation.** Sum of intercepted beam <= DNI*cos(gamma)*(length of the union of silhouettes); transmitted + reflected = intercepted; absorbed <= transmitted <= intercepted; array diffuse <= isolated diffuse (ratio below one). The bound is on the silhouette union, not the horizontal footprint, because cylinders legitimately intercept more than the footprint irradiance at low sun.
5. **Culture limits.** kappa -> 0: absorbed -> DNI*kappa*pi*R^2 (thin limit); tau large: absorbed -> transmitted. n = 1, normal beam: absorbed equals an independent `scipy.integrate.quad` of the chord integral. With `n = 1`, no wall and sea radiance equal to sky radiance (a full 4-pi isotropic field), isolated-tube absorbed equals the 170 `cylinder` field integral (continuity with T1 to the stated quadrature tolerance); a normal beam at `t2` hand-off gives `I0_eq = DNI`, `f_d = 0`, and the 172 growth rate equals T1 at that `par` to 1e-12.
6. **Probe Q1 reproduced.** Isolated daily energies (739, 582, 294, 390 Wh/m) reproduce within 0.5 % (no wall, no refraction; the isolated case has no shading assumption). The array values of Q1 are not a pass criterion because Q1 uses a single-row rule; the kernel is compared with it on a single row and the differences, with reasons, are tabulated in the evidence. New cases: pitch sweep, two and three layers, N-S vs E-W, both solstices.
7. **Regression.** With `optics_model` absent or `t1`: 170 golden table, 172 series, manifests and digests byte-identical to master (pinned). Determinism: same inputs give byte-identical arrays. Fail-closed cases of decision 8, NaN or negative DNI/DHI, invalid layout (module larger than `tube_count`, pitch < D, more than 64 module tubes), `n < 1`, `rho` outside [0, 1].
8. **Performance (measured, recorded).** A 120-day, 5-minute run with 24 tubes adds at most 3 s of optics at `prepare` and no measurable `rhs` cost (prototype: 0.12 s sun vector + 0.82 s shading); 48 tubes at most 10 s (prototype 5.3 s). The test records wall time on the host and gates at 2x the measured prototype to absorb machine noise. The existing T2 budget (<= 30 s per simulated month) is not exceeded.
